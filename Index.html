How a repair moves

Five steps, from your operatory back to it.

The same sequence runs whether the fault is a blown fuse or a full rebuild — only the parts and hours change.

01

Diagnose

Call, WhatsApp, or send a video of the fault. Most issues get a preliminary diagnosis and quote within the hour.

Response window: < 60 min, 9am–8pm IST, seven days a week.
02

Pickup

Within Kerala, a technician collects the unit directly. Outside Kerala, we arrange insured courier pickup at no cost to you.

Coverage: Kochi & Ernakulam same-day visit · rest of India via partner courier.
03

Repair

Work happens on the bench at our Kochi lab, using OEM or OEM-equivalent parts — never generic substitutes.

Parts stock: 420+ SKUs held on-site for common failure points.
04

Calibrate & test

Every repaired unit runs a load test cycle matched to its equipment class before it's cleared to ship.

Autoclaves: 3 full cycles logged · Compressors: 2-hour pressure-hold test.
05

Return & warranty

The unit ships back with a calibration certificate and a written warranty covering the repaired component.

Standard cover: 90 days parts & labour on the repaired fault.

Common questions

Before you call.

Do you repair dental equipment outside Kerala?+

Yes. Our lab is based in Kochi, Kerala, but we arrange insured courier pickup for clinics anywhere in India. Regional turnaround typically ranges from 48 to 96 hours depending on the zone.

How long does a typical repair take?+

Average turnaround is 48 hours from pickup to shipment, though simple fixes like handpiece bearings can return within 24 hours and larger rebuilds may take up to 96 hours.

Is there a warranty on repairs?+

Every repair carries a 90-day warranty on parts and labour for the specific fault that was fixed, along with a calibration certificate.

What equipment does Menlo Healthcare repair?+

We repair autoclaves and sterilizers, air compressors, dental chairs and delivery units, micromotors and handpieces, RVG sensors and imaging equipment, and LED curing lights and ultrasonic scalers.
